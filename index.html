false perspective

sweet yet mysterious
personal yet recognisable

False Perspective plays with a straightforward honesty. No disguises, no detours. Their songs are intimate with layers which reveal themselves one by one. There’s room for doubt, for sharpness, for everything that’s felt but not immediately spoken.

Live, they perform as an acoustic ensemble: Ugnė (vocals), Liz (guitar, vocals), Hugo (clarinet, saxophone), and Eline (violin, vocals). A small group that fills the room.

In 2026, their UNTOLD/UNFOLD tour is interwoven with the release of three EP’s. The first one, Idioglossia, has been released in February.

False Perspective has been nominated for the Rob Acda Awards 2026 and the PopDelft Talent Award 2024. They have performed at venues such as Patronaat, TivoliVredenburg, Het Paard, Bibelot and De Kroepoekfrabriek.
